## Goal

Grow **neuropsychological assessment** capacity so the practice can **pick and choose** referrals — instead of taking everything.

Today about **80%** of assessments come through **lawyers / insurance** (medico-legal). Those files are heavy (large record packages, many hours, strict paperwork). That work can continue, but it should not be the only growth path.

**Dr. Sylvie Sauriol** wants more **private clients** who request assessment **through the website**, without the lawyer–insurer route. We will **test** that channel for 30 days and measure results.

## Hard rules (different from normal marketing)

- No client testimonials, review asks, or “family stories” in ads or posts.
- No cold messages to individuals or families who might need care.
- Organizational outreach (clinics, schools, SLPs) to a professional contact is different and allowed when done carefully.
- Titles stay exact: **Dr. Sylvie Sauriol, Psy.D.** · **Sebastian Jose, MPsy, MTh, RP (Qualifying)** — never “RPQ,” never imply Sebastian is the psychologist.
- No case details, diagnoses, or record-volume stories in public content.
- **No AI face/voice clone** of either clinician. Videos = slides + real voice (optional corner camera).
