### Post 1 — What a neuropsychological assessment is

A neuropsychological assessment is a comprehensive evaluation of cognitive, emotional, behavioural, and everyday functioning. It may include interviews, standardized testing, questionnaires, record review, behavioural observations, feedback, and a written report.

This post is general information only. It is not a professional opinion about any individual situation.

Complimentary 15-minute consultation: https://neuropsychologicalassessments.com/contact/

### Post 2 — Private assessment vs medico-legal (process only)

Some people contact a practice directly when they have questions about attention, learning, memory, or related concerns. Other assessments are requested through legal or insurance pathways and follow that process.

If you are unsure which path applies, a brief consultation can help clarify next steps.

General information only — not advice about a specific case.

https://neuropsychologicalassessments.com/assessments/
